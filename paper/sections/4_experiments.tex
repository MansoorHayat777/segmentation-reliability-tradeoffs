\paragraph{General scenes.} COCO val2017~\cite{lin2014coco} is evaluated through
the 20 PASCAL foreground categories; only images with eligible components enter
complete groups. All 1,449 VOC validation images~\cite{everingham2010pascal}
and 2,000 ADE20K validation images~\cite{zhou2017scene} are used. COCO and VOC
use six torchvision models~\cite{long2015fully,chen2017rethinking,
howard2019searching}. ADE-S contains SegFormer~\cite{xie2021segformer}; ADE-X
contains UPerNet ConvNeXt and Swin~\cite{xiao2018unified,liu2022convnext,
liu2021swin}, BEiT-B~\cite{bao2022beit}, and Mask2Former~\cite{cheng2022mask2former};
ADE-M contains recent Mask2Former, OneFormer~\cite{jain2023oneformer}, UPerNet
ConvNeXt-L, and BEiT-L checkpoints, fixed before any was evaluated.

\paragraph{Street scenes.} Cityscapes validation~\cite{cordts2016cityscapes} and
KITTI semantic segmentation~\cite{geiger2013kitti} are evaluated with six
Cityscapes-trained SegFormer checkpoints. The priority map assigns 5 to
persons, riders, motorcycles, and bicycles and 4 to cars, trucks, buses, and
trains. KITTI was not used to train any model.

\paragraph{Abdominal CT.} We evaluate the \NImagesAmos{} labelled validation CT
volumes of AMOS~\cite{ji2022amos} with six public models:
TotalSegmentator~\cite{wasserthal2023totalsegmentator} at full and fast
resolution, MONAI~\cite{cardoso2022monai} whole-body models at high and low
resolution, Swin-UNETR~\cite{hatamizadeh2022swinunetr} trained on BTCV, and a
DiNTS multi-organ model, each run with its published pipeline. None is
documented as trained on AMOS. Scoring uses the five organs all six models
predict, with a clinically motivated priority map (pancreas 5; gallbladder and
spleen 4; liver 3; stomach 2), components of at least 1~mL, and a
three-dimensional port of the scorer that reproduces the two-dimensional one
exactly.

\paragraph{Protocol.} Class priorities, component eligibility (16 pixels in 2D),
the high-error threshold, and the frozen formulas are fixed before each panel is
evaluated. Checkpoint revisions are pinned, and re-running inference for the
four original general-scene panels and Cityscapes reproduced all stored
records exactly.
